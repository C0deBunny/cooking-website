Values are the ones drawn above. Colours are Tailwind semantic tokens, never raw hex.

Why a band: every other page on the site opens with one — this is the established page-shell pattern, not a new idea. It also gives the title its own surface so nothing is overlaid on a photo.

2 · Hero object — photo + stat bar

One bordered element, not two

Container
bg-card, 1px border, rounded-[18px], overflow-hidden
Photo
square, aspect-square, full width, never re-cropped
Stat bar
4 equal columns, border-t, hairline between cells
Cell
icon 18px + stacked value / label, centred
Value
15px, weight 800, tabular-nums
Label
9px, tracking-[0.18em], uppercase, muted

Why welded: a separate facts card read as a third floating box. Sharing one border makes the photo and its numbers one object — and it removes a card from the page.

3 · Ingredients card

Tickable — the one interactive part

#ingredients-heading — keep it

Why a child component: RecipeArticle is a server component on two routes and compiled into the wizard's client bundle. The tick state lives in a small "use client" list; the article itself must not gain useState.

4 · Method card

Why filled and not outlined: outlined circles read as empty checkboxes next to the real ones in the card above. Filled removes the collision. The note becoming a tinted callout stops it reading as an afterthought.

What RecipeFallback must become. Same geometry as the page above, so the swap is invisible.

Load-bearing details, easy to undo by accident

  • The two heading ids stay. #ingredients-heading and #steps-heading are the navigator's targets and the wizard rail's scroll targets. Rename either and one breaks silently.
  • RecipeArticle gains no useState and no server-only import. It renders as a server component on two routes and is compiled into the wizard's client bundle. Ticking lives in a child.
  • The square is never re-cropped. No full-bleed hero, no object-cover into a band — the stored file is the framing its author chose.
  • The wizard's live rail keeps today's look. Only the Review & Publish preview follows this design.
  • The skeleton ships with it, or the page flashes the old layout on every cold load.
